Process

Clear steps, clear deliverables. We prioritize actions that improve margin, consistency, and guest experience.

  1. Discovery — Quick intake + goals, constraints, and current challenges.
  2. On-site / Remote Audit — Workflow, menu, purchasing, staffing, service, and numbers.
  3. Plan — Prioritized roadmap with targets and timelines.
  4. Execution — Systems, training, tools, and weekly checkpoints.
  5. Stabilize — Maintain standards and keep performance improving.

About Chef Alejandro Porben

Chef Alejandro Porben is a Cuban chef with 14+ years of experience and multiple successful restaurant openings. He has helped operate and elevate high-performing concepts including Soho House, Amalfi Llama, Sparrow, Scarpetta, and others.

With a background across Spanish, Italian, French, Mediterranean, Japanese, Latin Caribbean, and healthy cuisine, Alejandro’s consulting is built on disciplined execution—improving restaurant performance through financial structure, BOH/FOH SOPs, training systems, and leadership accountability.

Over the last 4 years, consulting evolved from side work into a focused practice after consistently improving operations and impacting profitability through systems that teams can actually follow.

FAQ

Do you work with new openings or existing restaurants?

Both. We support new concepts (pre-opening to launch) and existing operations (profit, systems, consistency, turnarounds).

How fast can we see impact?

Typically within weeks once the highest-leverage fixes are implemented (menu engineering, purchasing, prep systems, training).

Is this remote-only?

Remote is available, but on-site audits are recommended when operational execution is the primary issue.
