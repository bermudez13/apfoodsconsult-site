Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AP Food Consulting (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you visit apfoodconsulting.com or contact us regarding restaurant and culinary consulting services.

Information we collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us through our website or by email. This information may include:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Restaurant or business name (optional)
  • Preferred language (optional)
  • Details about your restaurant, project, or consulting needs

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data. Please do not submit confidential or unnecessary personal information through the contact form.

Automatically processed information

Our hosting and security providers may process limited technical information such as IP address, request metadata, and browser information for purposes of site security, performance, and spam prevention. This information is not used for marketing or profiling.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and communicate with you
  • To evaluate potential consulting engagements and service requests
  • To schedule consultations or follow up on discussions
  • To maintain site security and prevent abuse

How we share information

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Information may be shared only with trusted service providers necessary to operate this website and deliver communications (such as hosting, security, and email delivery services), or when required by law.

Data retention

We retain contact information and communications only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, evaluate business opportunities, and maintain operational records.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels for marketing purposes. If analytics or tracking tools are added in the future, this policy will be updated before such tools are activated.

Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information submitted through the website. However, no internet transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by contacting us. Requests will be handled subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.
